<objective>
Prepare the live corpus for the backfill: stop the daemon, take a fresh backup, enable the ONNX embedder,
and take the pre-backfill measurement that proves Plans 12-01 and 12-02 actually work on real data.

This is Locked decision 1 in `12-CONTEXT.md` — the ONNX `bge-small-en-v1.5` embedder, not the hash
default. The hash embedder is keyword-shaped and is the direct cause of the two recall defects recorded at
Phase 11 sign-off (`11-SC7-SIGNOFF.md`, Q6 and Q12). Real semantic similarity is the point of the v1.1
milestone.

**Ordering is not arbitrary and every alternative produces a broken store.** Backfilling before enabling
writes 49k rows keyed `("deterministic","keyword-hash-v1")` that the ONNX missing set cannot see and
`findNearestSummaries` filters out — ~76 MB of dead rows with no cleanup path. Restarting the daemon
before the backfill completes gives new summaries ONNX vectors while the corpus has none, so retrieval
ranks over a ~0.1% sample. **The daemon stays stopped from this plan through Plan 12-07.**

Purpose: REQ-045; Locked decisions 1 and 2.
Output: `12-ENABLE.md` with backup evidence, the enable receipt, and the pre-backfill status snapshot.
</objective>
