<objective>
Rehearse the entire live operation on a clone before touching the live corpus, exactly as Phase 11 did for
every corpus write.

The estimates this phase has been planned against are extrapolations from a 300-summary sample:
24.2 ms/summary → ≈20 minutes for 49,314. `12-RESEARCH.md` Assumption A1 is explicit that this is a floor,
not a ceiling — the corpus holds 30 summaries over 100 KB and 668 over 10 KB, and a 521,320-char summary
measured 167 ms against 69 ms for its first 4,000 chars. This plan replaces the extrapolation with a
measurement.

It also tests the two properties Phase 10 claimed and GAP-12-A disproved — resume and idempotence — at
real scale, under the real embedder, which is the only way to know Plan 12-01 actually holds.

**One risk this rehearsal exists to find:** `backfill-embeddings-command.ts:68` loads summaries into a
single in-memory map before batching. Plan 12-01 narrowed that load to retrievable states, but 49,314
summaries with ~1.4 KB average text (one of them 818 KB) still all land in memory at once, Zod-parsed.
That has never been run at this scale. Measure peak RSS. If it is uncomfortable, that is a finding to
report, not to work around mid-run.

This plan touches NO live data. The daemon stays stopped from Plan 12-05.

Purpose: REQ-043, REQ-045; de-risks Plan 12-07.
Output: `12-REHEARSAL.md` with measured numbers.
</objective>
