<objective>
Take Plan 11-22's finding to the owner and get the one decision an agent cannot make: what the
acceptance target for GAP-11-E actually is, and therefore what shape the fix takes.

Two questions genuinely need the owner:

1. **Which acceptance target governs?** ROADMAP SC3 forbids `[Tool call:` argument text "from collapsed
   runs"; 11-CONTEXT.md's locked decision says a single isolated tool call keeps its existing text; Plan
   11-13 authored a stricter must_have — "no injection packet contains tool-call or harness-injected
   text" — which no requirement states. If the finding's offenders are largely H-0 (isolated calls
   retained by design), the "defect" is a disagreement between a plan's self-authored bar and a locked
   product decision. Only the owner can say which is wrong.
2. **Which remediation?** A rebuild over a 4.6 GB corpus is hours of compute and a destructive-by-way-of-
   supersession operation. If the leak is in the rules, the rebuild reproduces it.

Purpose: gate the remediation's shape on evidence plus owner judgement rather than on planner assumption.
Output: `11-REMEDIATION-DECISION.md` and the STATE.md record.
</objective>
