<objective>
Prove the phase's measured mechanism without the personal database, sweep the blast radius, and record the
decision. A CI-safe corpus at the measured class proportions is contracted twice — once with hygiene
disabled at the rule-table level and once normally — and BOTH the summary-count and the volume reduction
are asserted. Then the full suite is swept for downstream fallout in boxes, traces, and retrieval, and
ADR 0048 is written.

Purpose: SC6's "materially falls" and SC7's "recall does not regress" are the two criteria that cannot be
proven by unit tests on the hygiene module. 11-MEASUREMENT's key finding is that an implementation can hit
the volume target and miss the count target; this plan is the automated guard against shipping that
variant.
Output: Ratio test + fixture + green blast radius + ADR 0048.
</objective>
