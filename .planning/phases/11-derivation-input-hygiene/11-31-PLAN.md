<objective>
Remove the Mission Control credential from git history. Owner decision 2026-07-28: rewrite the history.

**This plan runs LAST in Phase 11, after SC7 sign-off, and the ordering is deliberate.** Rewriting from
`00de5a3` forward gives new SHAs to **40 commits** — the 16 that carry the credential and the 24
descendants. Every SHA cited in a planning doc written before the rewrite becomes dead. Doing this after
sign-off means the sign-off's own citations are the last ones that need remapping, rather than being
invalidated by a later step.

Facts established before this plan was written:
- `git-filter-repo` is available at `/opt/anaconda3/bin/git-filter-repo`.
- `00de5a3..HEAD` is 40 commits.
- Nothing is pushed: `main` is **400 commits ahead** of `forgejo/main`. No coordination cost, no
  force-push to a shared branch, no collaborator rebases.
- **84 SHA-shaped tokens** appear in `.planning/` and `docs/` markdown. Not all are commit references —
  some are content hashes and event ids. They must be distinguished, not blanket-replaced.

The risk here is not the credential. It is that this repository IS the work product of this phase, and a
botched rewrite loses it. Every safeguard below exists for that reason.

Purpose: close GAP-11-F's history half.
Output: `11-HISTORY-REWRITE.md` with backup evidence, the commit map, and the verification sweep.
</objective>
