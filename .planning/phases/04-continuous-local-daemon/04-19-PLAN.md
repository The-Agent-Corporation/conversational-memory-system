| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Guarded stage result -> scheduler completion | A stale owner must not mutate health or due state after its stage commit is rejected. |
| Lease turnover -> loop shutdown/reconstruction | Old-owner cleanup must not release or overwrite replacement-owner state. |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-04-19-02 | Tampering | Scheduler.completeWork | critical | mitigate | Persist outcome atomically under fresh token before changing ephemeral queue state. |
| T-04-19-03 | Denial of Service | turnover recovery | medium | mitigate | Reconstruct rejected work under the replacement owner and prove exactly-once convergence. |
</threat_model>
