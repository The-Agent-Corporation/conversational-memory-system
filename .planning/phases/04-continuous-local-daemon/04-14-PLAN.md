<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Durable lease -> long-running stage | Writer authority must stay fresh while local source/derivation work is awaited. |
| Status snapshot -> operating-system signal | PID reuse or lease turnover must not target another process. |
| Source grants -> watchers/scheduler | Only exact active grants may be read or watched in the background. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-14-01 | Elevation of Privilege | DaemonLoop lease heartbeat | critical | mitigate | Independently renew exact token, assert freshness at stage boundaries, latch loss, and never release another token. |
| T-04-14-02 | Spoofing | daemon-command signal seam | high | mitigate | Re-read current lease immediately before SIGTERM and match unexpired token plus PID. |
| T-04-14-03 | Tampering | grant/watch synchronization | high | mitigate | Reconcile from durable approved grants and synchronize the exact watcher set; no background approval mutation. |
| T-04-14-04 | Information Disclosure | diagnostics/tests | medium | mitigate | Synthetic histories and injected time/OS seams only; no raw content, secrets, results, or unapproved paths. |
</threat_model>
