<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Durable grant registry -> daemon scheduler | Only exact approved person/candidate/scope grants may become runnable background work. |
| Person A registry -> person B registry | Reconciliation must never enumerate, preserve, or delete another person's source state. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-04-10-01 | Elevation of Privilege | reconcileDaemonSourceStates | high | mitigate | Derive rows inside the store from current approved grants; do not accept caller-supplied grants or approval state. |
| T-04-10-02 | Tampering | source-state preservation/removal | high | mitigate | Preserve only exact grant/person/candidate/scope matches and transact the SQLite replacement atomically. |
| T-04-10-03 | Information Disclosure | tests and diagnostics | medium | mitigate | Use synthetic fixtures and content-free state; exclude source bodies, raw errors, secrets, and unapproved locators. |
</threat_model>
