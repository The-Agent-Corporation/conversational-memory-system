<objective>
Close the two reproduced SC3 exact-parity divergences from 08-VERIFICATION.md so the phase's "exact match, not closely enough" guarantee actually holds under malformed and edge-case input, and fold in the cheap, parity-relevant review warnings (WR-01 codec endianness/alignment, WR-03 locale-dependent tie-break, WR-04 silent score-0 on dimension drift, WR-05 non-transactional delete).

Purpose: Phase 8's whole design driver is byte-identical results between SqliteStore and InMemoryStore. Two public-interface-reachable inputs currently diverge — a NaN-scored row under minimumScore (kept by SQLite, dropped by in-memory) and a dimensions/vector.length mismatch (silently served by in-memory, throws on read in SQLite and poisons the whole partition). The parity test suite never exercises either. This plan makes the two stores identical by construction and adds the missing test coverage.

Output: hardened codec + both stores fixed identically, new edge-case parity tests guarding the divergences, full suite green.
