<objective>
Close CR-02 (verification GAP-2, correctness, BLOCKING): the per-device envelope hash chain is produced one way and verified another. `sealOne` sets `header.previousEnvelopeHash` from `hexToBase64Url(predecessor.sealedEnvelopeDigest!)`, where `sealedEnvelopeDigest = sha256HexOfBase64(envelopeBytesBase64)` — a SHA-256 over the whole base64 envelope JSON. The verifier (`ReplayVerifier`, per its own documented contract) requires `previousEnvelopeHash` to equal the predecessor's `ciphertextDigest` — a BLAKE2b generic hash over the ciphertext bytes only. The two digests differ in algorithm and input, so every device-sequence >= 2 is rejected `SYNC_INTEGRITY_FAILED` before decryption. No device can replicate more than one operation; REQ-034 offline convergence fails for any real history, and REQ-035 tombstone propagation rides the same broken pipeline.

Purpose: Make the producer chain on the exact value the verifier checks so multi-operation histories converge.
Output: A one-line-semantics producer fix plus round-trip and convergence tests that currently fail and then pass.
</objective>
