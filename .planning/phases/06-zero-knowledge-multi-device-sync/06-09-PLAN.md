<objective>
Close CR-01 (verification GAP-1, security, BLOCKING): account recovery can currently enroll an active device without possessing the recovery seed. The relay verifies the recovery-enrollment authorization against the CANDIDATE DEVICE'S OWN signing key, and the ADR-specified `CMSRSIG1` recovery signing credential is never derived. Possession of the relay account alone (learn `packageId` via `recovery.fetch`, self-sign) is therefore sufficient to become an active device — directly violating REQ-032 and ADR 0047 D-04/D-16 "Account Recovery Cannot Enroll or Decrypt."

Purpose: Bind recovery-seed possession into the enrollment authorization so account access alone can never enroll or (via future epoch packaging) decrypt.
Output: A recovery signing credential derived from the seed, persisted with the active package, and verified by the relay; an adversarial test proving account-only enrollment is rejected.
</objective>
